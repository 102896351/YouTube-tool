At Vidora we try to collect as little as possible. This page explains, in plain language, what information is involved when you use the tool at vidora.tools and the mirrored pages at 102896351.github.io/YouTube-tool, and what we do with it.

1. The short version

You do not need an account to use Vidora. We do not ask for your name, phone number, or payment details. We do not sell your information and we do not build advertising profiles about you.

2. What we collect

When you submit a link, the helper service necessarily sees the URL you entered and technical details of the request (such as your IP address, browser type, and the time). That information is used to fetch and deliver the file you asked for and to keep the service stable.

If you email us, we keep the message and your reply address so we can answer you.

3. How a request is handled

The link you paste is sent to our small backend, which looks up the available formats and returns a temporary download URL. The resolved file is delivered directly to your browser. We do not permanently store a history of the videos you download, and we do not use your requests to build a profile.

4. Cookies and analytics

The site may use basic analytics to understand how many visitors use the tool and which pages are popular. Where analytics are enabled, they are aggregated and not used to identify you personally. We do not place advertising cookies. You can block cookies in your browser at any time; the core download function does not depend on them.

5. Who we share with

We share information only in these limited situations:

  • with the hosting and network providers that keep the site and helper service running;
  • when we are legally required to disclose something;
  • to protect the rights, safety, and integrity of the service and its users.

We do not sell or rent personal information. Occasional analytics or infrastructure providers act only on our instructions.

6. How long data is kept

Request metadata is kept only as long as needed to serve the request and maintain logs for troubleshooting and abuse prevention. Emails are kept until the conversation is resolved. Temporary download links expire on their own.

7. Your choices

You can use the tool without giving us contact details, decline or clear cookies, and ask us to delete any email correspondence you have sent. To make a privacy request, email hello@vidora.tools with a short description. We will reply within a reasonable time.

8. Children

Vidora is intended for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the tool changes or as the law requires, and will revise the date at the top. For questions, requests, or concerns, write to hello@vidora.tools. Related reading: our Terms of Service and the Contact page.
